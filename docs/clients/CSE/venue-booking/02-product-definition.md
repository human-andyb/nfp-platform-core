# Product Definition

## Vision

Create a modern venue booking experience that allows clients to:

- Discover the venue
- Understand options and pricing
- Check availability
- Submit bookings
- Pay deposits
- Receive automated communications

while enabling CSE staff to manage bookings through Dataverse.

---

## Core Principles

### Configuration Driven

Venue setup should be configurable.

### Reusable

Capability should support future clients.

### CRM Integrated

All bookings must be linked to:

- Contacts
- Accounts
- Communications
- Payments

### Incremental Delivery

Launch core capability first.

Enhance over time.

---

## Primary Personas

### Venue Hirer

External customer booking space.

### Venue Administrator

Internal CSE staff member managing bookings.

### Finance

Tracks payments and reporting.

---

## Success Measures

- Reduction in manual administration
- Increase in venue enquiries
- Increase in venue bookings
- Booking to payment conversion
- Accurate CRM records
